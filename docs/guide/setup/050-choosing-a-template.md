![The template picker, showing several starting points side by side with their own colours and lettering.](shot:template-picker "Each one is a different structure, not the same page recoloured.")

A starting point is a complete site: a palette, a type pairing, page structure, and sections
already arranged and filled with placeholder words.

## They differ in structure, not just colour

This is the part worth knowing. No two of them share a composition, a type personality, a
product-card treatment and a motion profile — that is a rule the codebase actually enforces,
not an aspiration. Recolouring one would not turn it into another.

So choose on **shape**: how the top of the page works, how products are laid out, how much air
there is. The colours are the easiest thing to change afterwards and the structure is the
hardest.

:::warning
You can change your starting point later, but it replaces the page you have. Anything you
already wrote into a section goes with it. Swapping early costs nothing; swapping after an
afternoon's work costs the afternoon.
:::

## What you can change afterwards

All of it. The palette, the lettering, the corner rounding, how much space sits between
sections, which sections exist, what order they are in, and every word.

:::faq
### Will my shop look like other shops using the same one?

Only until you change something. The words and the photographs are the shop, and those are
yours from the first minute.

### Can I start from a blank page?

Not from the picker — starting points exist because a blank page is a bad place to begin. But
you can delete every section from one and be left with something very close to blank.

### Can I look at one before choosing?

Yes. The [templates page](/templates) shows all of them full-size, without signing up.
:::
