Click anything in the canvas and the right-hand column becomes its settings. Click something
else and it becomes that thing's settings instead.

There is no settings page with forty rows where thirty-six do nothing. What you see is what
applies.

## The kinds of control

**Words.** Type into the box and the canvas updates as you type. There is no save button; see
[undo and version history](/guide/builder/undo-and-versions) for what happens to it.

**Choices.** Layout, alignment, how many columns, which corner rounding. These are a small set
of named options rather than free numbers, on purpose — "2, 3 or 4 columns" cannot produce a
broken layout, and "any number you like" can.

**Switches.** Show prices, show the search, keep the header on screen while scrolling. On or
off.

**Pictures.** Opens your media library, or uploads a new one. See [pictures](/guide/selling/pictures).

**Tone.** Most sections offer a background: the page colour, or a raised one. Alternating them
down a page is what stops it reading as one long scroll.

## Why the choices are named

Look at the colour controls and you will find nine **roles** rather than a colour picker —
"Buttons", "Highlights", "Page background". Changing the one called Buttons changes every button
on the shop, and they stay consistent with each other because they were never separate.

A free-for-all gives you a shop with four slightly different oranges in it and no way to find
them all again.

:::note
This is why a shop built here is hard to make ugly. The controls are deliberately not a design
tool — they are a set of decisions somebody already made, with the parts you would actually want
to change left open.
:::

## Per-section notes worth knowing

:::faq
### The hero's height

Compact, tall or full. Full means the first screen is the picture and nothing else, which suits
a shop with one very good photograph and suits nothing else.

### Product grid: which products

All of them, the newest few, or a category. "Newest" is the one to pick if you add things often
— the page rearranges itself and you never touch it again.

### Prices can be turned off

Per product grid. A grid with prices off and a contact section below it is a catalogue that
takes enquiries, which is the right shape for anything made to order.

### The announcement bar

Part of the header. A line of text across the very top. Empty means it does not render at all,
so clearing it is how you remove it.
:::
