Three columns. Sections on the left, the page in the middle, and on the right the settings for
whatever you have selected.

On a phone the two side panels become sheets that slide up over the page, because three columns
on a phone is three things too narrow to use.

## The middle is not a preview

Worth saying plainly, because most builders are not like this: the page in the middle is drawn
by exactly the same code that draws your live shop. It is not an approximation that gets
re-rendered properly later.

There is no second implementation to drift, which means there is no category of bug where the
editor showed you one thing and the shop published another.

## The left panel

Four tabs.

| Tab | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Add** | The sections you can put on the page |
| **Layers** | The whole page as a list — reorder, hide, jump to one |
| **Pages** | The pages of the shop, including ones you cannot delete |
| **Assist** | Ask for a change in words, and get a proposal |

## The right panel

Whatever you clicked on. Select a heading and it offers the heading's words, its size and its
alignment. Select a product grid and it offers how many columns, which products and whether
prices show.

Only the controls that apply to the thing you selected — there is no settings page with forty
rows where thirty-six do nothing.

:::tip
Click on empty space, away from every section, and the right panel becomes the theme: the
palette, the lettering and the shapes for the whole shop at once.
:::

## Undo

Everything is undoable, and the steps are the size you would expect. Typing a heading is one
step, not one per letter.

:::note
Nothing in the builder is live. You are editing a draft; the shop the public sees does not
change until you publish. There is a Preview button for seeing the draft as a customer would,
without publishing it.
:::
