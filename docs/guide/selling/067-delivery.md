![The shipping page: zones, and the rates that apply inside each.](shot:shipping "A zone is where you post to. A rate is what it costs to get there.")

Two ideas, and they nest.

A **zone** is somewhere you post to — "India", "Tamil Nadu", "everywhere else". A **rate** is an
option a customer picks inside that zone — "Standard, ₹60", "Express, ₹150".

## Why this blocks publishing

A shop with no delivery option has a checkout nobody can complete. The customer fills in their
address, reaches the delivery step, and there is nothing to choose — so the order cannot be
placed and they leave.

That is why it is a blocker rather than a warning: it is not a shop that looks unfinished, it is
a shop that cannot take money.

:::tip
If everything you sell is digital, you still need one zone with one free rate. Give it a name
like "Nothing to post" and price it at zero.
:::

## The three kinds of rate

| Kind | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Flat** | The same amount however much is in the basket |
| **Free** | No charge |
| **Free over** | Free above a threshold, a flat amount below it |

**Free over** is the one worth thinking about. "Free delivery over ₹2,000" reliably raises the
average basket, because somebody at ₹1,700 will find another ₹300 of things rather than pay ₹80
of postage. Set the threshold somewhere above your typical order, not below it.

## Several rates in one zone

Offer more than one and the customer chooses. "Standard, 5 days, ₹60" beside "Express, 2 days,
₹150" lets somebody in a hurry pay for it.

:::warning
Name them for what the customer gets, not for the courier. "Express — 2 days" means something;
"Blue Dart Surface" does not, unless your customers are logistics people.
:::

## What the customer sees

At checkout, after the address — because which zone applies depends on where it is going. The
total re-adds itself as they change the choice.

:::faq
### Can delivery be free for some products and not others?

Not per product in this build. The rate applies to the basket.

### What if I only post within one city?

One zone covering it, and no other zone. An address outside it then has nothing to choose, which
tells the customer you do not post there — clearly, and before they have paid.

### Do I have to charge what it costs me?

No, and most shops do not. Postage is part of the price for many small makers; a free rate with
the cost built into the product price is a perfectly normal choice.
:::
