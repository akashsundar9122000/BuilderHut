:::danger This is not tax advice
What follows explains which switches exist and what they do. Whether you should be charging tax
at all, at what rate, and what you must show on an invoice depends on where you are, what you
sell and how much you turn over. Ask an accountant. Getting this wrong is expensive in a way
getting a heading wrong is not.
:::

## Inclusive or exclusive

The one decision that changes what customers see.

**Inclusive** — the price shown is the price paid. Tax is worked out backwards from it and shown
as a line on the receipt. This is what shoppers expect in India and most of the world.

**Exclusive** — tax is added at checkout. The total goes up after the customer has decided. Normal
for business-to-business, and a reliable source of abandoned baskets for anything else.

:::tip
If you are selling to the public, choose inclusive. A basket that grows at the last step is the
single most common reason people leave one.
:::

## Rules

A rule is a rate that applies somewhere. Most shops need one: the rate that applies where they
are. Add more if you sell into places that tax differently.

A rule can be switched off without deleting it, which is what you want when a rate changes or a
threshold moves.

## GSTIN and HSN

Both fields exist. **GSTIN** is your registration number and appears on invoices. **HSN** is the
classification code for a product, set per product.

:::note
Neither is required to run a shop. If you are below the registration threshold, leave them
empty — the shop works exactly the same and the invoice simply does not carry them.
:::

## How tax is worked out

On the server, every time, from the rules as they stand. Never in the browser and never stored
on the product.

The reason is that an order records what was actually charged, as whole paise, at the moment it
was placed. Changing a rate next month does not rewrite last month's orders — those say what
they said. A figure worked out in the shopper's browser could disagree with the server's, and
the first place anyone would notice is the payment step, which is the worst possible place to
find out.
