## The name

What a customer sees, and what they search for. "Ridged stoneware vase" finds more people than
"Vase 03".

## The description

Whatever somebody needs to know before they are willing to buy. What it is made of, how big it
is, how to look after it, how long it lasts.

Blank lines make paragraphs. There is no formatting beyond that, on purpose — a product
description that needs headings is usually two products.

:::tip
Write the answer to the question you are tired of answering. "Holds water; the glaze stops
short of the foot so the body shows" does more work than "beautiful handmade vase".
:::

## The price

Typed in whole currency units — `2400` is ₹2,400. Prices are kept exactly, as whole numbers of
paise, so nothing rounds and nothing drifts between the basket and the receipt.

### A compare-at price makes a discount

Set one higher than the price, and the shop strikes it through, works out the percentage, and
shows it. You do not calculate anything.

:::warning
A compare-at price should be a price you actually charged. Inventing one to manufacture a
discount is, in many places, illegal — and customers notice.
:::

## Draft, active, archived

| Status | On the shop | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Hidden | Something you are still writing |
| Active | Visible and buyable | Everything you sell |
| Archived | Hidden, and kept | Something you no longer make |

Archived rather than deleted, because an old order still points at the product it was for. A
deleted product would leave an order describing something that no longer exists.

:::faq
### Can I sell the same thing in several sizes?

Put the sizes in the description for now if they are the same price. Separate prices means
separate products.

### Do I have to set stock levels?

No. Leave stock alone and the shop simply never says "out of stock".

### What if I make everything to order?

Turn prices off for that section and use a contact button instead. See [the questions at the
start](/guide/setup/the-questions).
:::
