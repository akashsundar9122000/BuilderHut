If you would rather see the whole thing than read about it, this is a recording of the
product doing everything this guide describes. Nothing in it is a mock-up or a storyboard:
it signs up, answers the onboarding questions, adds a catalogue, builds a home page section
by section, publishes, and then buys something from the shop it just made.

![A recording of BuilderHut, from signing up to a customer signing in to find their order.](video:builderhut-demo "The whole product, in one run. Subtitles are on by default — the narration and the captions are the same words.")

## A note on the last chapter

The end is worth watching even if you skip the middle. Somebody checks out as a guest, with
no account — which is what most people do. Later they come back and make an account with the
same email address, and the shop does **not** simply hand over what it is holding. It asks
for a code first, because that record already has their address, their phone number and what
they bought on it, and typing an email address is not proof that it is yours.

That rule holds whatever you set under [Store settings](/guide/running/the-dashboard); it is
not a preference. Everything else about accounts is yours to choose — whether people sign in
with an email address or a mobile number, whether they use a password or a code, and when you
want their details confirmed.
