Everyone who has completed an order, with what they bought and when.

## What is here

A name, an email address, the orders they placed, and what they spent. Click through to any
order.

That is the whole of it, and the absence of more is the point.

## What is not collected

:::note
No browsing history tied to a person. No profile built across shops. No "customers who looked at
this also looked at that".

Visitor counting uses a **first-party cookie scoped to one shop** — a random value, with nothing
joined to a customer record and nothing shared with any other shop on BuilderHut. So your
analytics can tell you how many people visited, and cannot tell you that a particular person
did.
:::

That is a deliberate trade. It means slightly less information for you, and it means a customer
buying a cake is not being followed around.

## Customers are per shop

Somebody who buys from two BuilderHut shops is two customers, one in each. There is no shared
identity across the platform, so your customer list is genuinely yours and nobody else can see
it — including other merchants and including us, beyond what running the service requires.

## Contacting people

The email address is there. There is no bulk mail tool in this build — see
[reminders](/guide/running/orders) for the one automated message the shop sends, which is about
an order somebody did not finish paying for.

:::warning
Somebody giving you an email address to complete an order has not agreed to a newsletter.
Adding them to one is, in most places, against the law and, everywhere, a good way to be marked
as spam. The newsletter section on your shop exists so people can ask for it.
:::
