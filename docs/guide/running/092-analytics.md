## What is counted

**Visitors** — distinct people, by a first-party cookie scoped to your shop, over a rolling
window.

**Sessions** — a visit. Somebody who comes back tomorrow is one visitor and two sessions.

**Orders** and **revenue** — as on the dashboard, net of refunds.

**Conversion** — orders divided by sessions, as a percentage.

## Today and history are stored differently

Raw events are kept briefly and rolled up nightly into daily totals, which are kept.

The practical consequence: **today's figures move as the day goes on**, and yesterday's are
final. If a number changes between the morning and the evening, that is the day still happening
rather than a fault.

## How much to read into it

:::warning
Early on, almost nothing. Ten sessions and one order is a 10% conversion rate and it is noise —
the same shop next week will show 0% or 25% with nothing having changed.

Counts are honest at any volume. Percentages start meaning something somewhere around a few
hundred sessions a month. Until then, read the counts and ignore the rates.
:::

## What the numbers cannot tell you

They cannot tell you **why**. A dip might be a broken picture, a holiday, or an Instagram post
that did well last week and is now buried.

:::tip
The single most useful thing is not on this page: open your own shop on your own phone, as a
customer, once a week. Add something to the basket and go as far as the payment step. Most
problems that cost sales are visible in thirty seconds and invisible in a chart.
:::

:::faq
### Where do visitors come from?

Referrer information is recorded where the browser provides it. A lot of traffic arrives with
none — anything from a messaging app usually does — so "direct" is larger than it really is
everywhere, not just here.

### Does this use Google Analytics?

No. Nothing is sent to a third party. That is also why there is no cookie banner: a first-party
counter scoped to one shop, with no profile and nothing shared, is not what those rules are
about.

### Can I see which products are viewed most?

Product page views are counted. Orders per product are on the products list.
:::
